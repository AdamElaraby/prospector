Prospector

The pipeline

Each step feeds the next. Everything is safe to re-run — nobody gets found, drafted, or messaged twice.

1

Find businesses

Pulls from Google via whichever source you picked in Setup. Leave the boxes empty to use every niche and city in Setup.

2

Audit and qualify

Checks what is technically broken, and separately what the site cannot do for a customer — booking, quotes, service information, a way to make contact. Then scores each business out of 100 and keeps the ones worth your time. No website on its own is not enough.

3

Write the messages

One message per lead, built from what the audit actually found. Costs a fraction of a cent each.

4

Send

Use WhatsApp. It opens each chat with the message already typed, needs no domain, no address and no account, and it is the only way to reach the businesses that have no website — your best leads. Email is optional and set up separately.

Drafts

Edit anything that reads wrong before it goes out. Changes save to the draft.

Revenue

Leads found is a number that only ever goes up. These are the numbers that decide whether any of this was worth doing. Nothing here is guessed — you record each step as it actually happens.

Funnel

How many leads ever reached each stage, and what share of the previous stage that is. The stage where the bar collapses is the thing to fix.

Deals

You

Your first name is the only thing messages need. You don't need a company, a domain, or an address to offer someone a website — leave the rest blank and the messages read as one person, which is the honest version and the one that gets replies.

unsaved changes

Email

Optional, and off by default. Email needs a domain you own, and US law (CAN-SPAM) requires a real postal address in commercial email — that applies to individuals too, not just companies. A home address or a cheap PO box both count. WhatsApp needs none of this, so you can leave this whole section alone.

API keys

Stored in .env, readable only by you, never sent anywhere but the service they belong to. Leave a box blank to keep the existing key.

What to look for

This matters more than any other setting. Trades where a website isn't assumed convert; dentists and law firms don't.

How picky to be

Every business is scored out of 100: is it trading, is there a real problem, can it plausibly pay, and could you actually build what it needs. Having no website is not a qualification on its own — a closed shop has no website either. Raise the bar to get fewer, better leads.
